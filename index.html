Listening Parties

Same song, same second

Start a party, share the six-letter code, and up to four friends listen along. Songs they don't have are sent to them for the party and removed after.

Lyrics

Lyrics that keep time

Timed lyrics are found for you, word by word where they exist. If a song has none, time them yourself in a few taps.

Lucid mode

Full screen, full colour

Lyrics and visuals that move with the music and take their colours from the cover.

Tools

Find any song's key and tempo, fix titles and covers, and clean up duplicates.
