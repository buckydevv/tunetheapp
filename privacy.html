Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Your music and listening history stay on your PC. This is what Tune! stores online when you make an account, and why.

What we store

  • Account: your email and password (stored encrypted), so you can sign in.
  • Profile: username, name, bio, pictures, and who you follow. Other Tune! users can see these.
  • Listening summary: your top artists and songs and total listening time this year, shown on your profile.
  • Listening now: the song you're playing, shown to people who follow you. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Parties and shared playlists: which you're in, and the song titles added to shared playlists.
  • Reports you send about other profiles.

In a Listening Party, songs a guest doesn't have are shared with the party and deleted when it ends. Party chat isn't saved.

Who handles it

Supabase (accounts and data), Brevo (sign-in emails) and Cloudflare (this website and the anti-spam check on sign-up). They only process it for us. We don't sell your data or show ads.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. You can do that any time in Tune! under Account, and everything above is deleted with it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@tunetheapp.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the ICO.

Tune!'s online features are for people aged 13 and over.
