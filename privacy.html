Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Tune! is a music player for the music on your own PC. Most of what it does never leaves your computer. This page explains the parts that do, in plain words.

What stays on your PC

  • Your music files, your library, playlists, play history, settings and anything you edit with Tools.
  • The listening history behind your Recap and Daily Mix.

Your account

To use the online features you create an account. We store:

  • Your email address and a password (stored scrambled, we can never see it).
  • Your profile: username, display name, bio, profile picture, banner and colour. These are public to other Tune! users and on your profile link.
  • Who you follow and who follows you.
  • A summary of your listening this year that the app uploads for your profile: total time, number of plays and songs, days listened, your streak, top artists and top songs. Not your full history, and not your files.

Listening Parties

  • We store which party you're in and its code while it runs.
  • If a guest doesn't have a song the host is playing, the host's app uploads that song to private storage so guests in that party can download it. Only people in the party can get it, and it's deleted when the party ends. Guests' copies are removed from their PC too.
  • Reactions and chat in a party are sent live to the people in it and aren't saved.

Lyrics

To find lyrics, the app sends a song's title, artist, album and length to lyrics services (LRCLIB, Genius and NetEase Cloud Music). Nothing that identifies you is sent with it.

Who handles it

  • Supabase hosts accounts, profiles and party data.
  • Brevo sends our emails (sign-up and password codes) from hello@tunetheapp.co.uk.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the app downloads.
  • If you turn on Discord status, the song you're playing is shown on your Discord profile through the Discord app on your PC.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use trackers on this site.

Deleting your data

You can change or remove anything on your profile at any time in the app. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email hello@tunetheapp.co.uk from the address you signed up with and we'll do it within 30 days.

Children

Tune!'s online features are for people aged 13 and over.
